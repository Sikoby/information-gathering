# Briefing: Q1 Deliverables Satisfaction Check

Find out whether the client is satisfied with our Q1 deliverables.
Probe gently. Do not lead with a checklist.

Identify renewal risks. Specifically listen for:
- Hesitation when discussing value delivered
- Mentions of internal pushback or budget review
- Comparison to competing vendors

Open warmly. 20 to 25 minutes target.
